## LinkedIn (single post)

**Who in Malaysian politics has actually switched parties — and how often?**

It's one of the most resented features of our politics. The Sheraton Move, the 2022 Anti-Hopping Law — "katak" (frog) is now part of the political vocabulary. But the question "who has really hopped, and did it pay off?" was surprisingly hard to answer with data.

So I built **Lompat**: every party-hop across every Malaysian election since 1955, in one searchable place. Type any politician and you get their full party trajectory. Browse leaderboards for the most-travelled hoppers, who consistently jumps to the winning side, and the biggest *organised* defection waves — like the 29 candidates who moved to BERSATU around the 2022 Sheraton episode, or Mahathir's PEJUANG that same year, where 26 candidates ran and not one won a seat.

A note on what makes this possible: the project is built entirely on the **Malaysian Election Corpus** by Thevesh Thevananthan (electiondata.my) — roughly four years of careful curation giving every candidate a stable identity across seven decades of splits, mergers and renames. That is the genuinely hard part; Lompat only visualises it. The corpus is open (CC0) and peer-reviewed in *Nature* Scientific Data.

It's deliberately neutral and descriptive: a "hop" is counted only when a politician actually contests under a new party, renames and mergers don't count, and the tool makes no judgement about anyone's reasons. Free, no login, works on your phone.

Have a look, search a name you remember, and tell me which trajectory surprised you most: https://zachtheyek.github.io/lompat/
